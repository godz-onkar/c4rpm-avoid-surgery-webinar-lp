Design

The C4RPM webinar look (the Reclaim and Avoid Surgery pages), on a cleaner layout.

FontOpen Sans everywhere: headings 700 to 800, body 400 at 18px (17px on phones). Remove the Inter, DM Sans and Space Grotesk the live page mixes in.
ColorsHeadings #000 · Body #333 · Teal text #1D6A85 (eyebrow, "Avoid Surgery:", numbers, quote name) so it reads clearly on the light blue bands · Teal #2682A1 (lines and borders) · Buttons #23799A, a shade darker than the live #2682A1 so white text passes contrast · Photo blocks and dots #2B9FCF · Hero and reviews band #D8ECF5 · Dr. Lewis band and form band #E8F8FD · Closing band and sticky bar: teal gradient #145A70 to #1E7896
ButtonsEvery button says Save My Free Seat, uppercase, 10px corners, and scrolls to the form. White button on the dark closing band and sticky bar. Nothing links off the page.
Signature detailsDr. Lewis's photo sits on a blue offset block (as on the live webinar pages), a dashed countdown pill, a LIVE badge in the sticky bar, numbered rows with a teal 01 to 05, and the logo ring as the highlighted stop in the problem diagram.
