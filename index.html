Build notes

  • Layout preview (in review). godz-onkar.github.io/c4rpm-avoid-surgery-webinar-lp shows desktop and mobile side by side, with these build notes under the frames.
  • Deadline. Live by Thursday, Nov 20. The Dec 1 ads go live Nov 21.
  • Design. The C4RPM webinar look (the Reclaim and Avoid Surgery pages) on a cleaner layout:
    • Open Sans everywhere. Remove the Inter, DM Sans and Space Grotesk the live page mixes in. Sizes, desktop / phone: headline 44 / 30px ("Avoid Surgery:" line 22 / 17px), section headings 34 / 24px, card titles 18 / 16.5px, body 17 / 16px, the date line 16 / 15px, form labels and the form card's top line 15px, small print 14px, eyebrow 12 / 11.5px.
    • Spacing: sections 72px top and bottom on desktop, 48px on phones; 28px from a section heading to its content (20px on phones); 48px between columns; content 1140px wide with 32px side margins (20px on phones). On phones every section lines up on the same 20px left edge, and every bulleted or dashed list indents its text 28px.
    • Three backgrounds, so no two neighboring sections match: hero #D8ECF5 → card white → problem #E8F8FD → learn white → how it works #D8ECF5 → Meet Dr. Lewis #E8F8FD → reviews #D8ECF5 → fit filter white → form #E8F8FD → FAQ white → closing band dark teal. Each tinted band gets a 1px top border in rgba(20,90,112,.12).
    • Headings #000, body #333. Teal text (eyebrow, "Avoid Surgery:", numbers, quote name) in #1D6A85 so it reads clearly on the light blue bands; #2682A1 for lines. Buttons #23799A (a shade darker than the live #2682A1, so white text passes contrast), uppercase, 10px corners. Cards 14px corners. Photo blocks, dots and the quote mark #2B9FCF. Closing band and sticky bar in a teal gradient, #145A70 to #1E7896, with white buttons.
    • Cards use a soft shadow or a thin border, never both, and no colored stripe down one side.
    • Headings in title case, as typed above. The copy is otherwise exactly as written above.
  • Images. Dr. Lewis's headshot from the live page (GHL media library), with its blue frame cropped off: hero, Meet Dr. Lewis, the form card and the phone hero. A second photo of him would help for Meet Dr. Lewis if the clinic has one. White logo in the closing band. No stock photos: the arm exam in the hero, the doctor and patient in the learn section and the DNA background come off.
  • One version of each section. The live page has hidden desktop and mobile copies of every section, with different testimonials. Delete the duplicates so desktop and mobile show the same copy.
  • Sticky bar. Teal gradient, "Free webinar · Tue, Dec 1 · 7 PM Pacific" on the left and a white button on the right (desktop, fixed to the top); full-width button only (phone, fixed to the bottom). No "LIVE" badge: the webinar is pre-recorded. It appears once the hero button has scrolled off the top and hides while the form or the closing band is on screen. This needs a custom code block: use the sticky-bar script from the preview's page.html (marked "Sticky bar"), and give the hero button the ID hero-cta, the form section register, and the closing band the class final.
  • Hero. Light blue band. Logo line at the top. Eyebrow, then the headline in two sizes: "Avoid Surgery:" small in teal, the rest large in black. Subheadline. Then the countdown pill and the date on one line, the button under them, and the proof line (five stars, the Google rating, "Board-certified pain physician") under the button. The page never mentions a replay. Desktop: copy left, Dr. Lewis's photo right on a blue offset block, with his name and "Your presenter" over the bottom of the photo. Mobile: no big photo; a round headshot with his name and "Your presenter" sits under the subheadline. On a 390px phone the eyebrow, headline, subheadline, date and button land on the first screen.
  • "This is for you if" card. A white card with a soft shadow that overlaps the bottom of the hero: the four lines in two columns on desktop. On mobile it runs edge to edge in one column, with a hairline on top.
  • Countdown. GHL's built-in countdown can't do this (it only ticks days, hours, minutes and seconds), so use the countdown script from the preview's page.html as a custom code block. It reads "N days left" down to two days out, then "Tomorrow at 7 PM Pacific," then "Today at 7 PM Pacific," and hides after the session. The date is set in one line at the top of the script (EVENT), in Pacific time, so daylight-saving changes don't move it. A dashed pill at the start of the date line, never flashing.
  • The problem (section 3). Text left; on the right, in a white card that lines up with the top of the heading, a simple three-stop line: physical therapy and cortisone shots, regenerative medicine (the highlighted middle stop, drawn as the logo ring), replacement. On mobile that button is hidden; the sticky bar carries it.
