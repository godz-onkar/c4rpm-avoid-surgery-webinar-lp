## 3. The problem

**Heading:** When Therapy and Shots Stop Helping Your Knee

Your knee aches going down the stairs and stiffens up after you sit. A long day on your feet costs you the rest of the evening.

You've given physical therapy a fair try. Cortisone shots helped for a while, but each one seems to wear off sooner than the last.

Now surgery is starting to look like the only step left. It's a big one to take before you know what else is out there.

In this webinar, Dr. Lewis shows where regenerative medicine fits between the shots and surgery.

## 9. FAQ

**Heading:** Common Questions

**I'm not great with computers. Can I still watch?**
Yes. Click the link in your email at 7:00 PM Pacific, and the webinar opens in your web browser on a computer, tablet or phone. There's nothing to download.

**Am I too old for this?**
No. Many of our patients are in their 60s, 70s and 80s. At a consultation, the physician looks at your joint and your overall health.

**Is this a sales pitch?**
It's a teaching webinar. At the end, Dr. Lewis walks through the $299 in-office consultation for anyone who wants a physician to look at their knee. There's no obligation to book.

**Can I sign up for a parent?**
Yes. Choose "A family member" on the form. One registration covers everyone watching on the same screen.
