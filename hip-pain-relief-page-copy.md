## 3. The problem

**Heading:** When Your Hip Pain Outlasts Therapy and Shots

Getting out of the car takes a push with both hands. By evening, a deep ache has settled into your groin.

You've done what you were told. Physical therapy helped for a while. Cortisone shots helped too, until each one wore off sooner than the last.

Waiting for a replacement has a cost. The joint keeps wearing in the meantime, and the longer it goes, the less of your own hip there is to save.

In this webinar, Dr. Lewis shows why the hip's limited blood supply makes it slow to recover on its own, and where regenerative medicine fits before surgery.

---

## 4. What you'll learn

**Heading:** What You'll Learn

1. **The four most common sources of hip pain,** and why an ache in the groin often starts in the hip.
2. **What platelet-rich plasma (PRP) and bone marrow concentrate are,** and how they differ from the cortisone shots you've had.
3. **Why aim and dose matter in a joint as deep as the hip,** and how image guidance helps an injection reach the right spot.
4. **What a limp does to your back and knees,** and why a bad hip rarely stays a hip problem.
5. **How the price compares with a hip replacement,** once you count the recovery time.
