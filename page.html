When the Shots Wear Off and Surgery Is Next

Stairs take a hand on the rail. Walks end sooner than you planned.

You've done what you were told. Physical therapy helped for a while. Cortisone shots helped too, until each one wore off sooner than the last.

Now the next step on the list is a replacement, and it's a big one to take without knowing what else is out there.

In this webinar, Dr. Lewis explains how regenerative medicine works with your body's own healing, so you can see whether it could fit your knee or hip before a surgery date goes on the calendar.

Meet Dr. Lewis

Dr. Jamie Lewis is board-certified in Physical Medicine and Rehabilitation and in Pain Medicine. He teaches at the Washington State University School of Medicine and the St. Luke's Physical Medicine and Rehabilitation residency program.

His work in regenerative medicine started at home. His wife hurt her hip about 12 years ago, and surgery left her with a year of pain. A concentrated platelet injection is what finally got her back to skiing and mountain biking.

"When you see that kind of result in someone you love, naturally you want to offer that same minimally invasive path to your patients so they can hopefully avoid surgery in the first place."Dr. Lewis

Common Questions

I'm not great with computers. Can I still watch?

Yes. Click the link in your email at 7:00 PM Pacific, and the webinar opens in your web browser on a computer, tablet or phone. There's nothing to download.

Am I too old for this?

No. Many of our patients are in their 60s, 70s and 80s. At a consultation, the physician looks at your joint and your overall health.

Is this a sales pitch?

It's a teaching webinar. At the end, Dr. Lewis explains the $299 in-office consultation for anyone who wants a physician to look at their knee or hip. There's no obligation to book.

Can I sign up for a parent?

Yes. Choose "A family member" on the form. One registration covers everyone watching on the same screen.
