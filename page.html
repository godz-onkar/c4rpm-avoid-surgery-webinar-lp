When the Shots Wear Off and Surgery Is Next

Stairs take a hand on the rail. Walks get cut short.

You've done what you were told. Physical therapy helped for a while. Cortisone shots helped too, until each one wore off sooner than the last.

Now the next step on the list is a replacement, and it's a big one to take without knowing what else is out there.

In this webinar, Dr. Lewis shows how regenerative medicine works with your body's own healing, so you can see whether it could fit your knee or hip.

Meet Dr. Lewis

Dr. Jamie Lewis is board-certified in Physical Medicine and Rehabilitation and in Pain Medicine. He teaches at the Washington State University School of Medicine and the St. Luke's Physical Medicine and Rehabilitation residency program.

His work in regenerative medicine started at home. His wife hurt her hip about 12 years ago, and surgery left her with a year of pain. A concentrated platelet injection is what finally got her back to skiing and mountain biking.

"When you see that kind of result in someone you love, naturally you want to offer that same minimally invasive path to your patients so they can hopefully avoid surgery in the first place."Dr. Lewis

About the clinic. Dr. Lewis and his team see patients in person at two clinics, in Spokane (South Regal Street) and Coeur d'Alene (West Hanley Avenue).
